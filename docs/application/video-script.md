# 2-minute video script (owner records screen + voice)

Spoken text is the plain paragraphs, about 280 words. Bracketed Russian lines are stage cues, not spoken.

[Экран: заставка с названием проекта, карта Азербайджана]

Hi, we are a team of two students. Our idea for food resilience in Azerbaijan is an early warning map for wheat drought.

[Экран: слайд с цифрой 55%]

In May 2026 the President said Azerbaijan is only fifty-five percent self-sufficient in wheat. Domestic output is about one and a half million tons, and imports are about one and a quarter million. When a dry season hits the harvest, that gap grows. We did not find an open, district-level view of where drought is building during the season.

[Экран: схема метода]

Our method is simple and explainable, no black box. For each district we take satellite vegetation greenness from MODIS, plus rainfall and temperature from ERA5, and measure how far this season departs from normal. We combine these into one drought index.

[Экран: таблица yields.csv, затем карта]

We compare it with official yields from the State Statistical Committee: sixty-six districts, six years. The map will show drought risk by district, next to the official yield anomaly.

[Экран: раздел Validation plan]

Here is the honest part. The back-test is not finished. We will leave out one year at a time, predict it from the other five, and report correlation, error, and how often we flag a bad year. If the signal is weak, we will say so.

[Экран: раздел Limitations]

The limits: only six years of data, one combined predictor, some liberated districts with unusual yields, coarse satellite pixels, and no field-level checks. No external organisations were involved. It is just the two of us.

[Экран: раздел Next steps]

Next, we finish the back-test, publish the numbers whatever they are, and build the interactive map. Thank you.
